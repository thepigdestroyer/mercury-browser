    Known gap in this build (an INFO line, not a failure): changing the start, reversed or value attribute of a list or an item after load does not re-number it, because those attributes do not ask the engine to lay the list out again. The page does it at the end and prints what it expects; the list above it keeps its old numbers until something else changes it.
