Actions

A screen reader reads the heading "Actions", then groups of controls: buttons, text fields, a slider and a spin button, a disclosure, a scroll area and a context target. Each control can be pressed, typed into, stepped, opened or scrolled to by the screen reader, and the page records what happened in window.log (events) and a few counters. The tests of crates/talaria-servo/tests/a11y_actions.rs press every action on it.
